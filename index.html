ABOUT THE LABORATORY

APPLIED SYSTEMS ENGINEERING & DETERMINISTIC RESEARCH.

Provision1 investigates how complex systems behave, where conventional operating assumptions fail, and how unexplored operating behaviors can be converted into repeatable engineering frameworks. We reduce complex systems to their fundamental elements and impose rigorous structural constraints governing information, phase, and signal behavior.

ACTIVE ENGINEERING PROGRAM

MEDIA SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

Independent engineering investigations into imaging, video, audio, and computational media systems. Our work examines signal behavior, processing architectures, and production workflows to develop deterministic engineering solutions for complex media environments.
