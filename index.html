Independent Research & Publishing

Research into complex subjects and systems.

P1Lab is an independent research and publishing practice investigating subjects that require sustained inquiry, evidence, reconstruction and interpretation. The work crosses physical systems, technologies, materials, places, cultural history and applied knowledge.

01 / Research

Current investigations.

Each investigation begins with a difficult question, an insufficient explanation, or a system that requires closer examination. Subjects are selected for the quality of the inquiry they demand, not for conformity to an industry category.

02 / Publications & Outputs

What research produces.

Investigation is carried through to a durable form. The result may be a publication, field record, technical study, research dossier, knowledge structure, physical system or documented body of evidence.

Monographs & Books

Research developed into books, monographs, field records, technical publications and extended studies intended to preserve and communicate difficult subjects.

Research Studies

Structured investigations documenting evidence, methods, observations, analysis and conclusions across technical, physical, cultural and historical subjects.

Technical & Knowledge Systems

Research translated into taxonomies, reference structures, documentation, application libraries, technical frameworks and other forms that make complex knowledge usable.

Physical & Applied Systems

Where appropriate, research proceeds into formulations, material systems, processing architectures, instrument studies and other physical or technical outcomes.

Research Archives

Primary datasets, field records, photographic records, physical taxonomies, source documentation, and structural research archives built across extended investigations.

Commercial Research Assets

Selected research may also produce application research, product architectures, technical references, editorial assets, or other defined intellectual property.

04 / Systems Practice

Technical practice.

Technical investigation and development across systems, materials, signals and physical processes.

Product as Platform

A commercial product is treated as a platform rather than a fixed category. The work investigates what the underlying thing actually is, what structures and states it contains, and what other useful food, material or industrial forms may be developed from it.

Technical Systems

Projects include deterministic media stabilization, signal conditioning, verification and audit systems, preservation-grade asset architecture, provenance infrastructure, transducer modeling and deterministic audio processing.

Investigate what is known. Examine what is assumed. Determine what can actually be said.
