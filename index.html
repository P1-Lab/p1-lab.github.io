P1Lab develops publications from initial research and editorial architecture through writing, editing, visual research, design, and production. We undertake selected commissions for organizations and individuals with a subject, history, collection, place, or body of work that merits a substantial publication.

Commissioned
Field Records.

Make your world visible. Preserve what makes it matter.

A documentary research and publishing commission for organizations with a distinctive world of work worth understanding and preserving.

P1Lab works within specialized businesses, workshops, facilities, and working environments to document the people, processes, materials, knowledge, and history that define them. Through field research, photography, interviews, and editorial development, that work becomes a considered, lasting record.

The result may take the form of a substantial printed publication, a researched archive, or a broader documentary record developed around the needs of the commission.
