Project overview

Production capabilities

Research through
finished work.

Documentary projects often require more than a camera or a script. P1Lab combines subject research, editorial development, visual documentation, publishing, and strategic communications experience, coordinating the right resources around each project's objectives.

Documentary & visual research

Subject development, documentary photography, interviews, location research, and the discovery of stories grounded in actual places and practices.

Editorial development

Research, narrative structure, writing, image selection, and the development of publications and documentary treatments with a clear editorial point of view.

Production & publishing

Project-specific production coordination, specialist resources, editorial design, book production, and preparation of agreed deliverables for their intended use.

Strategic Communications & PR

Public relations and communications for distinctive businesses and specialist industries. Media relations, campaign development, press materials, product launches, and editorial storytelling for agriculture, agritourism, food, hospitality, and suppliers.

An editorial property of P1Lab

FIELD

Remarkable work.
Worlds worth knowing.

FIELD is P1Lab's collection of monographs and guides documenting distinctive businesses, places, makers, and practices. Its publications combine visual documentation with research and editorial substance.

FIELD remains an independent publishing property and can also become part of a larger documentary commission, extending a project into a durable printed record.
