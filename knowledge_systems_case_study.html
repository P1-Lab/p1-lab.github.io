1. The Dealer Has A Different Problem

DEALERS DO NOT NEED MORE PRODUCT LITERATURE

Manufacturers provide specifications, manuals, demonstrations, recipes, videos, brochures, technical training, and application support.

Those resources explain the equipment. They do not necessarily identify the next commercial opportunity for the dealer.

A dealer may carry high-speed ovens, combi ovens, food processors, blenders, mixers, pressure-cooking systems, refrigeration, holding equipment, preparation systems, or multiple competing brands within the same category.

The commercial question is therefore much larger than: "What does this machine do?"

The better questions are:

Who else could use it? What problem could it solve? What process could it replace? What menu could it enable? What labor could it remove? What capacity could it unlock? And most importantly — could it create an entirely new revenue stream for the customer?

Provision1 develops the research and reasoning required to answer those questions.

9. Example Commission: High-Speed Oven / Café Revenue Expansion

Commissioned Application Study

A dealer sells high-speed cooking equipment to cafés and small foodservice operators. Those customers understand that the equipment cooks quickly, but many still view it primarily as a faster way to perform an existing task.

The dealer commissions Provision1 to investigate a different question: Can the equipment enable a meaningful new food or revenue program for an operation that cannot justify a conventional kitchen?

The resulting study could examine target customer profiles, footprint limitations, menu opportunities, workflow requirements, production scenarios, labor implications, equipment utilization, and potential new offerings.

The finished report could then become a dealer sales asset used to approach cafés that previously did not consider themselves candidates for the equipment.

The value is therefore not simply that the dealer knows more about the oven. The dealer now has a commercial argument for selling the oven to a different customer for a different reason.
