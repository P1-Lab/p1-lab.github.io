2. The Dealer's Real Problem

EQUIPMENT CAPABILITY IS NOT THE SAME AS COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY

Manufacturers explain what their equipment does. They provide specifications, manuals, demonstrations, recipes, videos, accessories, and technical training.

Your customer is asking a different question: What can this equipment do for my operation?

Can it let a café add hot food? Can a bakery increase production? Can a hotel create a new service? Can a commissary centralize preparation? Can a restaurant reduce labor? Can a convenience operation add a prepared-food program?

That translation from equipment capability → customer opportunity is often left to the individual salesperson.

THE MISSING COMMERCIAL LAYER
Provision1 turns that reasoning into a finished application asset your sales organization can use repeatedly.

3. Commission The Question

DON'T COMMISSION A REPORT. COMMISSION AN ANSWER.
The strongest project begins with a commercial question — not a request for generic research.

A dealer can bring us a product, customer segment, sales problem, or commercial opportunity. We define the research question and build the study around the answer required.

Market Expansion What other customer segments could use this equipment profitably?
New Revenue What new products, services, menus, or production capabilities could it create?
Labor / Workflow Where could this platform replace labor-intensive or inefficient processes?
Underused Capability What can this equipment do that customers are not currently being shown?
Customer Segment What would this platform look like when designed specifically for hotels, bakeries, cafés, healthcare, commissaries, or other operators?
Competitive Differentiation How can the dealer give its sales team a more compelling reason to recommend this platform?
