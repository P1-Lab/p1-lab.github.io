Product Knowledge Root™

The Root does not simply store information. It preserves the relationships, constraints, dependencies, and decisions required to derive correct operational outcomes from validated facts. While a fact may be "this amplifier outputs 500 watts," the relationship defined within the Root dictates: "Therefore, it cannot drive this speaker configuration above this specific impedance threshold."

Governance Ownership

Provision1 delivers the software system, operational environment, and core system nodes required to power your operational model; the organization retains ownership of its reality. Engineering defines technical reality, Regulatory defines constraints, and Product leadership defines operational intent. The Root preserves these decisions as an authoritative, version-controlled system block.

Not Another Knowledge Repository

Provision1 is not a document management system, search engine, or RAG-based AI chatbot. Those systems organize information. They do not determine which information is authoritative. A repository answers, "Where is the information?" Provision1 answers two questions: "What is the approved reality?" and "Who has authority to change it?" We eliminate the decentralized "invention of answers" that plagues scaling organizations.

Generative AI did not create the knowledge governance problem. It exposed it. An AI system without an authoritative model does not create intelligence; it creates a faster mechanism for distributing uncertainty. We provide the deterministic grounding required to ensure your AI interfaces remain bound to validated technical truth.
