Core Execution Parameters

  • Bilateral Interception: A pre-emptive, non-linear edge-preserving smoothing pass that executes on a dense luminance canvas before edge extraction, ironing out high-frequency surface "fuzz" (wood veneer grain, textile thread noise, JPEG blocking) while locking down true geometric boundaries.
  • Lattice Contrast Amplification: The mathematical calculation of localized spatial density deltas to isolate fine, repeating grid systems (such as cane webbing, gemstone settings, or structural masonry) without inducing Moiré distortion or aliasing noise.
  • Luminance Normalization: Deterministic normalization of directional shadows and specular reflections, ensuring that ambient environmental lighting changes do not warp or bloat the extracted physical dimensions of the object.
