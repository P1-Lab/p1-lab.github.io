Commissioned Field Records

Some businesses are worth documenting because of what they make. Others because of how they make it.

P1Lab produces commissioned field records of distinctive businesses, workshops, trades, facilities, and working environments—the people, processes, places, objects, and histories that ordinarily disappear without a record.

We work on location, around active operations, documenting the work as it actually happens. P1Lab brings photographic, documentary, research, editorial, and publishing capabilities together in a single field practice—converting active work into a permanent documentary record.

The subject comes first. The record follows.

“You have built something worth preserving. We document the people, work, place, process, and history that make it what it is.”

P1Lab Field Method Documentary Practice

This is not campaign photography. The purpose is to create a durable documentary record of the enterprise itself—its people, tools, physical environment, processes, objects, and history.

The subject changes. The method does not: observe the work, understand the system, identify the people and decisions, record the material evidence, and build the record.

02

What We Record

P1Lab documents six core dimensions of working enterprises, capturing both physical evidence and human knowledge.

01 / The People

The technicians, master craftsmen, founders, and operators whose tacit knowledge, hand-tool dexterity, and decision-making drive the enterprise.

02 / The Work

Active labor mechanics, skilled execution, assembly sequences, and physical transformation recorded as work actually happens on site.

03 / The Place

Working environments, physical plants, shop floors, yards, facilities, and the spatial architecture of productive operations.

04 / The Process

End-to-end workflows, material handling, tolerances, trade techniques, and engineering methods from raw input to finished state.

05 / The History

Institutional origin, site lineage, trade traditions, and oral histories recorded directly through structured conversations with key figures.

06 / The Objects

Finished builds, restored assets, high-value components, trade tooling, and physical artifacts documented in precise material detail.

Commission Structure

Engagements are structured around operational scope, research depth, and physical publishing requirements.

FIELD RECORD

FOCUSED SCOPE

COMPANY RECORD

FULL ENTERPRISE

A comprehensive record of the enterprise—its people, place, work, history, and finished output.

DEFINITIVE RECORD

Oral History & Trade Vernacular

A visual record alone is incomplete without the trade vocabulary, founder memory, and specialized working wisdom behind the shop floor.

During field work, P1Lab conducts structured audio interviews with key technicians, master craftsmen, and founders. These conversations capture trade terminology, company origin, critical engineering decisions, and institutional memory.

Preserving Working Wisdom

Capturing decades of unwritten technical expertise, shop adjustments, and historical craft knowledge before it is lost.

Editorial Integration

Recorded oral histories are edited directly into text blocks, captions, and narrative spreads within the published record.

07

The Published Record

P1Lab is an independent research, editorial, and publishing practice. Our work extends from physical field observation through technical writing, photography, and physical publication.

The primary product is the permanent record of the enterprise. The physical publication is its tangible, enduring manifestation.

Depending on commission scope, the record may encompass a master documentary archive, digital asset registry, or custom physical publication edition designed to sit in boardrooms, on founder desks, or in institutional archives as a permanent physical record of the enterprise and its work.
