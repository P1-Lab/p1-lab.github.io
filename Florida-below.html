The project / Natural history

A different view
of Florida.

Florida Below is a proprietary six-film natural-history documentary property originated, developed and owned by P1Lab for Florida Field & Ocean, P1Lab’s documentary programming initiative.

Each film explores an underwater environment through its wildlife, habitat and ecological relationships. The series is conceived as natural-history programming—not a tourism campaign or a product-led show.

The environments / Six-film structure

Six windows
below the surface.

The series is organized around distinct aquatic environments, from inland freshwater systems to coastal and offshore habitats. Final location selection and filming access will be confirmed during production planning.

Film 01 / Freshwater

Springs

Clear-water habitats shaped by constant flow, temperature and distinctive aquatic life.

Film 02 / Inland waters

Rivers

Living corridors connecting springs, wetlands, floodplains and the coast.

Film 03 / Coastal edge

Mangroves

Root systems and tidal margins that shelter aquatic life and connect land and sea.

Film 04 / Shallow coast

Seagrass Meadows

Underwater habitats that support marine life and contribute to coastal ecosystem health.

Film 05 / Offshore

Reefs

Complex communities where structure, current and species interactions sustain diverse life.

Film 06 / Submerged habitat

Artificial Reefs

Submerged structures examined as habitats and as part of Florida’s changing underwater landscape.

Production / Editorial / Delivery

One coordinated
production.

P1Lab originates and develops the property, establishes its editorial and commercial architecture, and assembles and coordinates the specialist capabilities required for production.

Filming is planned for January–April 2027. Site access, permits, technical requirements and location-specific conditions will be confirmed as production is finalized.

Florida Below is intended for release through Florida Field & Ocean’s documentary programming initiative. Distribution arrangements and partner usage rights will be defined separately.

The subject is Florida’s underwater life. Partner integration will be relevant to the work, clearly scoped and secondary to the documentary.
