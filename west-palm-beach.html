An annual field record of a working city.

West Palm Beach is not simply a collection of buildings, businesses, neighborhoods, waterways, and institutions. It is a working system: places, materials, trades, infrastructure, histories, services, commerce, architecture, memory, and the people who keep it operating.

P1Lab documents that system through a recurring annual regional publication. Each edition identifies and records a selected group of organizations, specialists, places, institutions, trades, and material realities that help define West Palm Beach.

The field remains.

01 / The Field

One city. Many systems.

West Palm Beach is the geographic field. The annual follows what that field contains and the relationships that make the city function.

A city is understood by following what actually happens inside it.

Research follows working waterfronts, construction, fabrication, marine activity, architecture, transportation, hospitality, food, infrastructure, landscape, preservation, commerce, institutional life, neighborhoods, and the material systems through which the city operates.

Organizations may enter the record because their work, facilities, collections, history, specialized knowledge, or physical presence establishes part of that larger system.

The annual is not a directory of businesses and not a tourism guide. It is an independent documentary record of the city and the evidence through which the city can be understood.

Research determines what warrants inclusion. Participation does not determine editorial conclusions.

02 / Volumes

One annual field. Two books.

Each annual edition retains the two-volume architecture because West Palm Beach requires enough physical space to treat both its working systems and its accumulated memory seriously.

Volume I

The Working City

Work, making, marine activity, construction, restoration, service, infrastructure, architecture, transportation, waterways, fabrication, hospitality, food, landscape, and the physical systems through which West Palm Beach operates.

Volume II

The Remembered City

History, neighborhoods, institutions, culture, architecture, collections, oral histories, preservation, migration, redevelopment, memory, property, civic identity, and the forces reshaping the city.

The division is editorial, not geographic. The two volumes follow the same city, and connections between them are expected.

04 / The Record

The city is the subject. The annual is the durable record.

The published books are the public form of a larger working documentary archive developed through field observation, photography, interviews, historical research, site documentation, and primary evidence.

Field Archive

Build the evidence.

Original field photography, interviews, research notes, historical material, maps, diagrams, site documentation, captions, source records, and other primary evidence may enter the working archive.

Annual Publication

Publish the record.

Each annual edition converts a selected body of that research into the physical public record of West Palm Beach. The books are edited from the evidence gathered in the field.

Institutional Record

Preserve what matters.

Participating organizations may separately acquire finished books, documentary photographs, interviews, archival material, or other agreed documentary outputs for institutional use, archives, offices, education, exhibitions, professional relationships, or long-term corporate history.

Continuing Field

Return next year.

The city does not become finished because one edition has been published. New businesses, facilities, projects, trades, histories, developments, technologies, specialists, and material evidence may enter subsequent annual editions.

The annual publication is one expression of the record. The fieldwork is the underlying work.
05 / Participation

The annual comes first. The city belongs inside the record.

P1Lab independently establishes the editorial record. Eligible organizations, businesses, institutions, specialists, and other participants may formally enter an annual edition through editorial listing and may contribute access, knowledge, materials, documentation, or additional documentary support.

Annual Editorial Listing

Be identified in the record.

Eligible businesses, makers, operators, marine organizations, trades, specialists, institutions, property-based enterprises, cultural organizations, and other participants may be formally identified within the annual record. The listing establishes a documented place within the field through relevant organizational, geographic, professional, or material identification. Participation does not determine editorial conclusions.

Access & Knowledge

Help establish the evidence.

Participants may provide access to working environments, facilities, equipment, collections, archives, historical material, technical information, firsthand knowledge, or knowledgeable people. Such contributions can strengthen the record while editorial control remains with P1Lab.

Commissioned Documentation

Document your operation.

Organizations may separately commission defined documentary work around their own history, facilities, working processes, architecture, machinery, archives, collections, neighborhood history, or specialized knowledge. Scope may include field research, photography, interviews, archival capture, sound, moving image, or other documentary work where appropriate.

Institutional Acquisition

Preserve the record.

Finished books, institutional editions, documentary photographs, interviews, recordings, archival materials, and other agreed documentary outputs may be acquired for archives, professional offices, clients, education, exhibitions, historical preservation, relationship development, or future research.

Participation establishes a place in the documentary record. It does not purchase favorable treatment, rankings, endorsements, advertising, or control over research conclusions.
06 / Annual

The city changes. The record continues.

West Palm Beach is not a finished subject. Each edition records another point in the continuing life of the city and creates a new opportunity for relevant organizations and evidence to enter the record.

Annual Edition

A continuing publication.

The West Palm Beach Field Record is conceived as a recurring annual regional publication. Each edition establishes a new documentary snapshot while retaining continuity with the field already examined.

Selected Participation

Defined representation.

Each annual edition identifies a selected body of organizations, specialists, places, institutions, trades, histories, and material systems relevant to that year's record.

New Evidence

The field keeps moving.

New companies, facilities, developments, trades, technologies, collections, neighborhoods, projects, histories, and working systems may enter subsequent editions as the city changes.

Two-Volume Architecture

The city gets room.

The annual retains the two-volume structure so that West Palm Beach's working systems and remembered realities can receive adequate space without compressing the field into a single publication.

One city. One continuing field. Another record each year.
07 / Method

Go there. Follow the evidence.

The record is built in the field rather than assembled from promotional material.

01 / Field

Observe.

Direct observation of places, operations, materials, facilities, infrastructure, collections, neighborhoods, and working environments.

02 / Photograph

Show the evidence.

Photography establishes physical conditions, objects, details, sequences, landscapes, architecture, machinery, workspaces, and visible relationships.

03 / Ask

Talk to people who know it.

Firsthand explanations from operators, builders, tradespeople, historians, owners, technicians, preservationists, institutional representatives, and other knowledgeable participants strengthen the record.

04 / Verify

Check the record.

Historical relationships, institutional claims, dates, physical evidence, and other material assertions are checked against available documentary sources wherever possible.

08 / Current Research

The field is fixed. The record is developing.

West Palm Beach is manageable. The city is not simple.

The geographic field is West Palm Beach. Research begins with the physical city and expands through the relationships encountered in the field.

Marine activity, fabrication, construction, architecture, restoration, hospitality, food, transportation, landscape, infrastructure, institutions, neighborhoods, collections, preservation, redevelopment, and specialized businesses may enter the record where their relevance and evidence can genuinely be established.

The annual is not intended to contain every organization in the city. It is intended to establish a meaningful, defensible record through a selected body of participants, places, systems, and evidence.
