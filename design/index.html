Provision1 Design Portfolio

Welcome to the Provision1 operational test matrix. For over 30 years, our studio has engineered premium visual assets for Tier-1 events, high-profile personalities, and global companies. The diverse creative works displayed below serve a dual purpose: they are both active market deployments and rigorous physical stress-tests utilized to calibrate our deterministic print conditioners and engines. By evaluating these complex graphic configurations across various challenging substrates—ranging from apparel and high-density spot-inks to large-format rigid and vinyl media—we ensure our technology continuously masters the complex laws of ink-set fluid dynamics and substrate limitations before files ever hit the shop floor.


AI generated images are labelled to show processing reports generated by our pre-press engines. The 4-Pane Forensic Audit Canvas is a visual diagnostic tool that breaks down an image file to ensure it can be physically printed without errors.

Pane 1 (Top Left): Shows the raw, unconditioned source artwork exactly as it was submitted by the client or designer. Pane 2 (Top Right): Displays the stabilized master file after our engine automatically mathematically adjusts the vectors and color channels for the intended substrate. Pane 3 (Bottom Left): Isolates a differential map to highlight hidden digital friction, such as unsafe color gradients or pixelated lines that would cause a printing press to stall. Pane 4 (Bottom Right): Simulates the exact physical behavior of ink deposition on the material, serving as an unassailable record that guarantees production fidelity before printing begins.
