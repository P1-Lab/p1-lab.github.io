GrüvType — Independent Documentary / Publishing

We document
worlds.

The things, places, people, processes, technologies, histories and knowledge that make a world what it is—and the permanent records that allow that world to be seen, understood and remembered.

The Practice

Everything has a material history worth keeping.

A garden. A countertop machine. A boat. A room. A bottle of wine. An American-made chair. An axe. A garment. A house. A place where something is made.

We look for worlds people already encounter, value or depend upon, but whose histories, materials, techniques and accumulated knowledge remain largely invisible.

Some of those worlds are changing. Some are disappearing. Some can only be properly understood while the people, places, machines and practices that constitute them are still here.

We go into those worlds and document them. The book is one principal, tangible and permanent expression of the record.

Editorial Position

A tradition of
looking closely.

GrüvType belongs to a tradition of publishing that treated the ordinary world as worthy of serious attention. People at work. Places as they actually are. Objects, materials and processes seen at close range.

Not as advertising. Not as decoration. As evidence.

The Difference

The world
comes first.

Not a catalog. We do not begin with a collection of participants and build a subject around them. The world exists first. We enter it and determine what belongs in the record.
Not a reference book. We are not simply organizing existing knowledge. We investigate a world while its evidence, places, objects and working knowledge can still be encountered.
Not advertising. Documentary access or institutional support does not determine the editorial subject, argument or outcome. The purpose is to document, not to manufacture promotion.
A permanent record. We identify the world, enter it, investigate it, and preserve what can be learned about the people, objects, places, processes and knowledge that make it what it is.
What Makes a GrüvType Subject

You already know it.
You just don't know the whole story.

The strongest subjects begin with familiar territory and open into a much larger world. The deeper question is whether that world is worth documenting while it exists.

Familiar

Something people already encounter, use, wear, inhabit, make or depend upon.

Hidden

History, materials, machinery, vocabulary, people or practices that are not immediately visible.

Living

A world that can still be entered, observed, heard and understood through its people, places, objects and working practices.

Worth keeping

Enough evidence, history, human knowledge and consequence to justify making a record that remains useful after the moment has passed.

What the record becomes.

A commissioned or institutional documentation project creates a defined body of research, fieldwork, photography, interviews, archival material and other documentary evidence. The objective is to preserve a world in a form that can remain useful after the circumstances around it change.

The book is often the principal published expression—but it is part of a larger record.

Documentary Record Research, fieldwork, photography, interviews, archival material and original documentation of the world itself: its people, places, objects, processes, history and knowledge.
Permanent Book A substantial physical volume giving the documented world a permanent, edited and distributable form. Something to retain, read, teach from, place in an archive, give to important people and return to years later.
Documentary Assets Where appropriate and separately agreed, original photography, interviews, audio, moving image, archival material or other documentary assets may be made available for institutional use or licensed separately.
Institutional Record A durable body of knowledge that can support institutional history, archives, exhibitions, customer relationships, dealer relationships, internal reference, education and future understanding of the organization or specialized world.

The value of the record is not dependent on selling enough copies to recover its existence. The record is made because the world itself is worth preserving. Finished books and other resulting materials are tangible expressions of that work.

Documentation does not require disclosure of proprietary formulas, confidential processes, customer information or unreleased material. We work with what is appropriate to document and publish, and company- supplied material is handled under agreed terms.

The editorial relationship remains independent. Commissioning or institutional support does not buy pages, favorable treatment, product placement or control of the finished publication.

GrüvType goes looking for the larger world while it can still be documented.

The Physical Book

One record.
Made permanent.

A GrüvType book is a field record you can keep: substantial, illustrated, carefully designed and intended to be read, handled, shelved and returned to.

It is not a brochure disguised as a book. It is not a catalog assembled around advertising inventory.

It is the physical embodiment of a much larger process of research, reporting, photography, observation and preservation.

A website can change. A campaign can disappear. A generation can retire. A book remains.

The Record

The record is larger than the book.

A GrüvType project begins with documentation. Where the subject calls for it, the record may include moving image, sound, interviews, process documentation, archival material, original photography and other forms of evidence.

The book is one expression of that record—the most substantial and permanent one—but not the only one.

The purpose is not simply to produce something that can be published. It is to leave behind something that did not exist in that form before.
