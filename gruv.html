We document
worlds.

The Practice

Some worlds disappear.
We document them while they are here.

A garden. A countertop machine. A boat. A room. A bottle of wine. An American-made chair. An axe. A garment. A house. A place where something is made.

We look for worlds that already exist around the things people encounter, value, use or depend upon—but whose histories, materials, techniques, people and accumulated knowledge are rarely preserved as a whole.

Some of these worlds are stable. Some are changing. Some are already disappearing.

We enter the world, investigate it, document it and return to it. Every GrüvType publication is conceived as an annual record: a continuing physical and documentary account of a world that can be enlarged, corrected, revisited and preserved over time.

Editorial Position

A tradition of
looking closely.

GrüvType belongs to a tradition of publishing that treated the ordinary world as worthy of serious attention. People at work. Places as they actually are. Objects, materials and processes seen at close range.

Not as advertising. Not as decoration. As evidence.

The annual format extends that tradition by allowing the record to continue: another edition, another year, another layer of evidence.

The Difference

The record
comes first.

Subject first. We begin with a world worth understanding, then identify the people, organizations, places, objects, systems and knowledge that belong to it.
Not advertising. Companies and institutions may belong to a documented world, but their commercial relationship to the work does not determine the editorial subject, argument or outcome.
Not a catalog. The purpose is not to display products. It is to understand the larger world in which those products, people, places and practices exist.
A continuing record. Each annual edition adds another layer of research, fieldwork, photography, interviews, objects, documents and knowledge to the record of the subject.
What Makes a GrüvType Subject

You already know it.
You just don't know the whole world.

The best subjects begin with familiar territory and open into a much larger documentary world. We look for subjects with enough depth, evidence and consequence to sustain an annual record rather than a one-time treatment.

Existing

A real world already inhabited by people, objects, places, systems, knowledge or working practices.

Vulnerable

Knowledge, machinery, places, trades, companies, techniques or histories that may change, fragment or disappear.

Useful

A record that can remain valuable as a reference, teaching aid, institutional resource, customer resource or object of continued study.

Continuing

Enough movement, depth and documentary possibility to justify returning to the subject year after year.

Annual Publication
For Companies & Institutions

There is a place
in the record.

The annual publication establishes a defined documentary field. Organizations that possess, steward or inhabit that world may participate in the record, contribute access and knowledge, acquire the resulting work, or commission deeper documentation of their own.

The annual editorial listing is the simplest form of participation: a formal identification within the relevant edition. Deeper documentary work remains available where the subject warrants it.

Annual Editorial Listing Eligible companies, organizations, specialists, makers, operators, institutions and other participants may be formally identified within the appropriate annual record through relevant organizational, geographic, professional, material or historical information.
Access & Knowledge Organizations may provide access to facilities, working environments, objects, archives, historical materials, knowledgeable specialists, technical information and other evidence that strengthens the annual record.
Commissioned Documentation A defined documentary undertaking may be commissioned around a company, institution, place, process or specialized world, including research, fieldwork, photography, interviews, archival work, sound and moving image where appropriate.
Institutional Acquisition Finished annual editions, documentary photographs, interviews, archival material or other agreed documentary outputs may be acquired as lasting institutional resources.
Continuing Participation Organizations may remain part of the record as the subject develops, allowing subsequent annual editions to document new products, places, processes, technologies, leadership, facilities, histories or other changes.

A participant is not purchasing editorial placement. Annual participation establishes a place in the documented world; it does not determine editorial conclusions, rankings, endorsements or favorable treatment.

The work does not require disclosure of proprietary formulas, confidential processes, customer information or unreleased material. We document what is appropriate to document and publish, and company-supplied material is handled under agreed terms.

The annual publication remains editorially independent. Participation, commissioning or acquisition supports the making and preservation of the record but does not control its conclusions.

Editorial Method

We follow the thing
until the world appears.

This is field reporting: research first. Then fieldwork. Then the objects, people, photographs, documents and unexpected connections that make the subject legible. The first edition establishes the record. Subsequent editions return to the field to document what has changed, what has endured and what has newly entered the world.

Research History, terminology, chronology, archives and primary sources.
Fieldwork Workshops, factories, kitchens, vineyards, ateliers, garages, nurseries, farms and other places where the subject exists.
Objects Tools, machines, materials, packaging and finished things as physical evidence.
Photography Original documentary photography showing things as they are made, used, maintained and handled.
Primary Evidence Manuals, catalogs, labels, drawings, photographs, documents and surviving records.
Knowledge The people whose expertise cannot be reduced to a specification.
The Unexpected Forgotten techniques, unusual uses, obsolete objects and discoveries that make the familiar unfamiliar again.
Return Annual revisits that record change, continuity, new participants and new evidence.

GrüvType goes looking for that larger world—and returns to it through the annual record.

The Physical Annual

A record you
can hold.

A GrüvType annual is a field report you can keep: substantial, illustrated, carefully designed and intended to be read, handled, shelved and returned to.

It can be a reference. An archive. A teaching aid. A customer or dealer resource. An exhibition object. A piece of institutional history. A record for the next generation of people who will want to know how the world worked.

It is not a brochure disguised as a book. It is not a catalog assembled around advertising inventory.

The physical annual is where the research, photography, reporting and accumulated knowledge become a permanent object that can be compared with the editions that came before and after it.

The Record

The record is larger
than the annual.

A GrüvType project begins with documentation. Where the subject calls for it, the record may include moving image, sound, interviews, process documentation, archival material, original photography and other forms of evidence.

The annual book is one expression of that record—the most substantial and permanent one—but not the only one. Subsequent editions can extend, correct, deepen and preserve the record further.

The objective is to create something worth keeping because the world it records is worth preserving.
