Florida Field Record · Working Waters · Jupiter / Palm Beach

Phlats
Inshore Fishing

A proposed field record of fishing, water, place, and accumulated knowledge on Florida’s working coast.

Florida Field Record is an independent monographic series documenting the physical, historical, cultural, ecological, and economic realities of Florida. Phlats can enter the series at two scales: as a substantial feature in Volume I, or as the subject of its own 80–100 page hardcover book.

01 / The proposition

This is a book about the work, not another piece of promotion.

The starting point is simple: Phlats already has a working practice, a body of accumulated knowledge, a particular relationship with the Jupiter and Palm Beach waterways, and years of experience that cannot be adequately represented by a website, social feed, or magazine article.

The record is the product.

P1Lab would document the operation in the field and build that material into a serious editorial publication: fishing practice, waterways, boat, equipment, weather, tides, technique, decision-making, clients, history, and the knowledge that comes from repeatedly working the same water.

The finished object is a substantial hardcover publication that can be owned, given to clients, placed with institutions, retained as part of the business archive, and distributed through the book trade.

This is not an advertising package and it is not a conventional fishing-magazine assignment. It is a commissioned publication within an independent documentary series.

Florida Field Record Books, not issues.

Each volume stands on its own as a substantial documentary investigation, while contributing to a cumulative record of Florida’s physical and working culture.

The physical book is the durable unit of the record.

03 / The field session

We go fishing.

The dedicated book begins with the actual operation rather than a staged portrait of it.

A 3–4 hour working trip.

P1Lab would join Craig on the water for a normal working session, documenting the operation as it actually unfolds: departure, water conditions, movement, equipment, tackle, technique, decisions, fish, clients where appropriate, and the physical environment in which the knowledge is applied.

  • The boat as working environment
  • Water, weather, tide, and seasonal conditions
  • Tackle and equipment in actual use
  • Technique and decision-making
  • Interaction with clients and the water
  • Knowledge that cannot be learned from a brochure
  • The ordinary physical details that make the practice distinctive

05 / The record

What would actually be documented?

The book would be discovered through the fieldwork. The categories below establish the territory without turning the project into a predetermined shot list.

01

The Water

Jupiter, Palm Beach, estuaries, inlets, tides, seasons, weather, currents, and the physical character of the fishing grounds.

02

The Practice

Boat, tackle, equipment, movement, preparation, technique, judgment, and the sequence of an actual working day.

03

The Knowledge

What years on the water have taught: patterns, decisions, exceptions, memory, observation, and accumulated local knowledge.

04

The Person

The experience and working philosophy of the person whose practice connects all of the preceding elements.

06 / Why the record

The reason to make the book is not that Phlats needs another advertisement.

Fishing magazines can cover a trip. Television can cover a personality. Social media can show what happened this week. None of those forms necessarily creates a permanent record of the accumulated practice behind the work.

Phlats operates within a particular Florida landscape and a particular body of local knowledge. That knowledge exists because someone has spent years reading the water, learning its changes, making decisions, and applying that knowledge for other people.

A field record gives that knowledge a physical form that can remain useful after the current season, current website, current social platforms, and current media cycle have passed.

For clients, it becomes a substantial object associated with the experience they have bought. For the business, it becomes part of its permanent archive. For the larger Florida Field Record, it becomes one documented piece of the state’s working culture.
