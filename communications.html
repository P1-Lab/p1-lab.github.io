Our approach

Substance before noise.

Effective public relations begins with understanding what makes an organization worth paying attention to. The work is to identify that substance, develop the story and bring it to audiences most likely to value it.

P1Lab draws on experience across publishing, documentary production, photography, editorial development and communications. Our perspective combines the discipline of editorial work with an appreciation for how businesses build recognition and reputation.

Engagements may be organized around a defined campaign or ongoing representation. Priorities, scope and deliverables are established around the needs of each organization.
