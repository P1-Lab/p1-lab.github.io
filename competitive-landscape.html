Why AI Changes the Equation

Before AI: A bad knowledge system creates slow searches, inconsistent answers, employee frustration.

After AI: A bad knowledge system creates automated misinformation, scalable hallucination, incorrect technical guidance, increased liability.

Generative AI did not create the knowledge governance problem. It exposed it. Without a validated product knowledge foundation, AI systems amplify ambiguity, outdated information, and unsupported assumptions. We provide the deterministic grounding required to ensure your AI interfaces remain bound to validated technical truth.

The Positioning Statement

Provision1 is not a better way to manage documents. It is not a documentation project, consulting engagement, or AI implementation layer.

It is the missing governance layer between product reality and every system that represents it. It is the governance infrastructure required to ensure that every future product interaction—human or machine—operates from an approved and validated understanding of the product.
