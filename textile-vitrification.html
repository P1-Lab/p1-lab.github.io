PROVISION1
Material-Bound File Vitrification for Digital Textile Production.

Digital image files are engineered for screens, not physical matter. When low-resolution web uploads or synthetic graphics hit a print line, they fail because they ignore the fluid dynamics of liquid ink on woven fabric.

The legacy choices are structurally destructive: vector tracing flattens organic texture into geometric polygons, while standard algorithmic upscaling ignores material physics—resulting in white underbase halos, capillary ink wicking, and shop-floor scrap.

PROVISION1 provides an automated ingest engine that stabilizes these assets at the gate. Operating entirely within the raster domain, it analyzes the file in linear light space to map and harden structural luminance boundaries before the file reaches the RIP queue. It preserves 100% of the artwork's original texture while enforcing vector-level ink containment.

Core Execution Parameters

  • Registration Trapping: Automated underbase choking along structural boundaries to eliminate white halos without line distortion.
  • Gradient Dispersal: Open-cell stochastic dithering to eliminate 8-bit digital banding and allow proper heat-cure outgassing.
  • Fluid Density Capping: Failsafe restriction of deep shadow tones to prevent substrate saturation and ink pooling.
Direct to RIP. Zero manual prepress.
