THE ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE CONTINUITY PROBLEM

The most valuable technical knowledge in an organization is often the least documented.

It exists in the diagnostic instincts of experienced engineers, the troubleshooting patterns of senior technicians, the practical knowledge of product specialists, and the undocumented decisions accumulated through years of system interaction.

This intelligence rarely exists in manuals, databases, or enterprise software systems. When critical personnel retire, change roles, or leave, organizations lose not only information, but the reasoning processes required to operate, maintain, and improve complex systems.

The result is repeated failure analysis, extended service resolution, inconsistent field decisions, and dependence on a small number of individuals who hold irreplaceable technical knowledge.

Provision1 extracts and structures engineering reasoning into governed technical intelligence systems that preserve institutional capability and improve operational continuity.
