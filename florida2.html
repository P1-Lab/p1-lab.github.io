Independent Monographic Series / P1Lab

Florida Field Record

An independent monographic series documenting the physical, historical, cultural, ecological, and economic realities of Florida.

Each volume is an independently conceived documentary work organized around a significant nexus discovered through field research. The subjects of future volumes are deliberately not predetermined. Research determines what warrants publication.

Every volume is produced as a substantial printed book designed for long-term reference, private ownership, and institutional collection. Together, the volumes form a cumulative record of Florida.

The subject comes first. The book follows the evidence.

01 / The Series

Books, not issues. Each volume stands on its own.

Florida Field Record is conceived as a continuing documentary library: independently researched books that accumulate into a larger record of the state without becoming a magazine, annual, or anthology.

Series Architecture

Independent monographs within a cumulative record.

Each volume is a complete work with independent value. A later volume does not supersede an earlier one. It adds another investigated reality to the record.

01 / Cumulative Architecture

One book becomes a library.

The series grows through independently conceived volumes. The continuing record is cumulative rather than sequential: every finished book remains useful in its own right.

02 / Open Research Directive

Research decides what gets made.

No rigid editorial calendar determines the subjects in advance. Field investigation, primary evidence, and discovered relationships determine what warrants a volume.

04 / The Nexus

Start with one reality. Follow everything connected to it.

The nexus is the structural mechanism of a volume. It is a specific physical, operational, ecological, economic, or historical entry point that opens into interconnected systems.

A single Florida place can contain an entire network of relationships.

The series does not begin by assigning a broad theme and collecting unrelated material beneath it. It begins with a real thing in the world: a working operation, a landscape, a waterway, a facility, a community, a material, an object, or a historical site.

Research then follows the relationships outward. Physical conditions connect to economics. Work connects to history. Infrastructure connects to ecology. Objects connect to people, place, and memory.

The result is a wider view of the subject without abandoning the original physical reality that made the investigation possible.

Agricultural Operation

An investigation originating in a citrus operation can extend into land ownership, water rights, soil cultivation, labor history, plant pathology, machinery, processing infrastructure, transportation networks, regional markets, and environmental transformation.

Working Waterfront

An investigation originating on a working harbor can extend into vessel design, commercial fisheries, shipyards, channel navigation, marine insurance, dock labor, coastal tourism, maintenance dredging, wetland ecology, and waterfront development pressure.

05 / Method

Go there. Observe. Verify. Build the record.

Reporting prioritizes direct observation, physical evidence, and primary knowledge over secondary synthesis. Research proceeds from field observation through verification, editorial construction, and publication.

01 / Field Observation

See the physical world firsthand.

Direct site visits and physical inspection. We examine infrastructure, operating routines, material conditions, spatial relationships, and the physical evidence present at the site.

02 / Primary Knowledge

Ask the people who know it.

Direct engagement with people who possess operational experience: crew members, engineers, agriculturalists, historians, mechanics, owners, and long-term workers.

03 / Verification & Evidence

Cross-check the record.

Field observations and claims are checked against public records, physical artifacts, technical literature, historical archives, maps, and other available evidence before publication.

04 / Editorial Restraint

Make reality legible without manufacturing it.

Prose and imagery are used to render physical reality visible with clarity and accuracy. The purpose is documentation, not promotion.

07 / Participation

Independent publication. Institutional participation. Editorial independence.

Participating organizations help make substantial field research and physical publication possible without purchasing editorial control or advertising space inside the book.

Florida Field Record operates under an institutional production participation model. A participating organization helps underwrite the field research and physical production required to document relevant work, expertise, places, projects, or operating systems within a volume.

Participation is not an advertising purchase, commercial product insertion, or manufacturing contract. It underwrites field reporting, original documentation, photography and visual preparation, image conditioning, editorial production, and hardcover manufacturing associated with the documented work.

The resulting publication remains an independently conceived and independently edited work. Participation does not purchase favorable treatment, authority over research, control over subject selection, or editorial conclusions.

The institution helps make the research book possible. It does not buy an advertisement inside it.

Regional Volume

Participation

Institutions may underwrite a defined editorial treatment within a forthcoming regional volume. Scope is established according to the subject, research requirements, documentary depth, and available visual material.

Extended Documentation

Expanded Record

More substantial subjects may warrant greater research, visual, technical, and documentary capacity. The appropriate scope is determined during the commissioning process.

Stand-Alone Work

Monograph

01 / Direct Agreement

Participation is bilateral.

Institutional participation and any associated allocation of finished books are established directly between P1Lab and the participating organization.

02 / Institutional Allocation

Copies can be reserved deliberately.

Participating institutions may reserve finished books for professional, client, archival, hospitality, or relationship-based placement under the terms of the direct agreement.

03 / Book-Trade Distribution

Separate from participation.

Independent retail and worldwide book-trade distribution operate separately from institutional participation. Neither channel alters the editorial independence of the publication.

04 / Editorial Independence

Underwriting does not determine the book.

The scope of participation determines research and production capacity devoted to the subject. It does not determine interpretation, conclusions, or favorable treatment.

Organizations considering institutional participation, production underwriting, or a stand-alone Florida monograph may inquire directly for current scope and production terms.
